How to research smartly

Look for pages with 10k–200k followers, not just huge celebrities. Their growth tactics are closer to what you can copy.
Open their Reels tab and sort mentally by views. The videos with views well above their usual are the formats to study.
Once you follow 5–10 good pages, Instagram’s “Suggested for you” will show more similar pages. Follow the best ones.
Create a separate Instagram account just for research, so your feed trains itself on fashion content only. Later this can become Mira’s account.
Save the best Reels into a collection called “Hooks” and another called “Formats,” and review them before you write scripts each weekend
